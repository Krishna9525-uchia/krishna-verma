## 2026-04-29 - [Copy to Clipboard Micro-UX]
**Learning:** Adding a "Copy to Clipboard" feature for calculation results significantly improves the utility of a calculator suite, especially when combined with immediate visual feedback (e.g., swapping icons) to confirm success.
**Action:** Always include temporary visual state (like a 2-second success icon) when implementing clipboard operations to provide clear affordance and feedback to the user.
