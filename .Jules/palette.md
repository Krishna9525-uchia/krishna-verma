## 2025-05-14 - [Copy to Clipboard Micro-Interaction]
**Learning:** Providing a temporary success state (approx 2s) after clipboard actions reduces user uncertainty. For accessibility, hidden buttons (opacity-0) must be made visible on focus (focus:opacity-100) to support keyboard users.
**Action:** Use 'group/result' and 'opacity-0 group-hover/result:opacity-100 focus:opacity-100' for secondary actions in cards to maintain visual cleanliness while ensuring accessibility.
