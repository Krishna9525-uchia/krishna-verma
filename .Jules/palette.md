## 2025-07-31 - Form Label Accessibility and Hit Target Polish
**Learning:** In dynamically generated form controls (such as list maps in React), omitting proper association between `<label>` and form inputs/selects (using `htmlFor` and `id` matched to a unique field identifier like `inp.name`) creates severe accessibility gaps for screen readers. Furthermore, applying `cursor-pointer` to labels associated via `htmlFor` provides immediate visual cue that the label is an interactive hit target, which focuses the associated input.
**Action:** When building custom calculators or data-entry screens, always ensure form labels are semantically linked to their corresponding inputs with `htmlFor` and `id`, and add a `cursor-pointer` class to the labels to elevate UX clarity and keyboard usability.
