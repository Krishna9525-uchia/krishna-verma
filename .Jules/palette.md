## 2025-05-23 - Accessibility Foundations in Calculator Apps
**Learning:** In utility-heavy applications like calculators, programmatic association between labels and inputs is often overlooked but critical for screen reader users and touch targets. Additionally, "Skip to content" links are essential for power users navigating via keyboard.
**Action:** Always check the relationship between labels and inputs in dynamic forms. Ensure icon-only buttons like mobile menus communicate both their name and their state (expanded/collapsed).
