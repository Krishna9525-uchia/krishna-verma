## 2026-05-16 - Copy to Clipboard for Primary Results
**Learning:** Users often need the final result of a calculation for use in other documents or communications. Providing a "Copy to Clipboard" button directly on the primary result card reduces friction. Using a 2-second success state (icon swap and ARIA label update) provides immediate, non-intrusive feedback.
**Action:** Always include a clipboard action for primary data points in utility apps, and ensure accessibility by updating `aria-label` dynamically.
