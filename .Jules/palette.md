## 2026-07-08 - Improving Feedback and Accessibility in Data Displays

**Learning:** Users often need to extract results from calculators for use in other documents. Providing a "Copy" button directly on the result cards significantly reduces friction. Additionally, ensuring that labels are properly associated with inputs (`htmlFor` and `id`) and have a `cursor-pointer` improves the perceived hit area and accessibility.

**Action:** Always implement a 2-second success state for clipboard actions to provide visual confirmation. Ensure all form controls have explicitly associated labels to meet WCAG standards and improve mobile usability.
