## 2026-04-03 - [Enhanced Result Interaction]
**Learning:** For utility-heavy applications like calculators, providing a one-click "Copy to Clipboard" feature with immediate visual feedback (icon toggle) significantly improves usability. Additionally, ensuring ARIA live regions are used for dynamic results is critical for screen reader parity.
**Action:** Always implement ARIA live regions for calculation outputs and provide copy-to-clipboard functionality with a 2-second feedback delay.
