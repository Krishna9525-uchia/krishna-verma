## 2025-05-14 - Copy to Clipboard for Results
**Learning:** Users often need to transfer calculator results to other documents (budgets, emails, reports). Providing a one-click copy button on the primary result card significantly reduces friction and prevents transcription errors.
**Action:** Always include a 'Copy' utility for primary data outputs in utility-heavy applications, ensuring visual success feedback (e.g., icon change) to confirm the action.
